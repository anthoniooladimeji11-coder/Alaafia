# Alaafia Expert Validation Form
## Nigerian Epidemiologist Pathway Rating Instrument

**Instructions:**
You will be shown a series of causal pathways proposed by the Alaafia multi-agent system from real NDHS 2024 records. For each pathway, rate it on four dimensions using the scale provided. Add comments where you disagree or see important nuance.

**Rater profile required:**
- Minimum 5 years experience in Nigerian public health research
- Familiarity with DHS/MICS survey methodology
- Knowledge of at least two of the six geopolitical zones

---

## Rating Scale
1 = Strongly disagree / completely implausible
2 = Disagree / unlikely
3 = Neutral / uncertain
4 = Agree / plausible
5 = Strongly agree / highly plausible

## Scoring Interpretation
- Mean score ≥ 4.0 across dimensions: Pathway accepted as high quality
- Mean score 3.0-3.9: Pathway accepted with revision notes
- Mean score < 3.0: Pathway flagged for architectural review

## Target Sample
Minimum 30 pathway records rated by minimum 2 independent Nigerian epidemiologists.
Inter-rater reliability calculated using Cohen's Kappa.
Target Kappa ≥ 0.60 (substantial agreement).
