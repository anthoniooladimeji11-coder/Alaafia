How to read this

Every figure is the state's own NG2024DHS survey estimate, shrunk toward a regression on geography in proportion to how noisy that state's own sample is (Fay–Herriot small-area estimation) — not the raw survey number, and not a guess. Basis says whether the margin of error came from the survey's own published confidence interval (api_ci) or an approximation (deff_approx) where it didn't publish one. Rank is among the other states this indicator could be fitted for that round — 1st is always the best outcome, regardless of whether that means the highest or lowest number. Full method, every documented approximation, and the LGA-level breakdown (synthetic, not locally measured) are in the estimates explorer and platform/model/README.md.
